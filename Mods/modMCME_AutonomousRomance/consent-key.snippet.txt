================================================================================
MCME Autonomous Romance - optional dedicated consent key (MCM_AR_Consent)
================================================================================

Consent already works out of the box - [C] on keyboard and [D-pad Down]
on gamepad (the potion on that button is suppressed while the prompt is
open; [D-pad Left/Right] also confirms via the sword-sheathe binding).
This snippet is ONLY for players who want consent on its own key while
sword sheathing stays where it is.

STEP 1 - declare the action (required):

  Open <The Witcher 3>\bin\config\r4game\legacy\base\input_qwerty.ini
  and paste this line inside the [BASE_DRAW_SWORDS_KEYBOARD] section
  (or [BASE_DRAW_SWORDS] for gamepad):

      IK_Z=(Action=MCM_AR_Consent)

  Z is unused by the vanilla game. If Z is already bound on your setup
  (check Options -> Keybindings first), replace Z with any free key
  name - IK_B, IK_V, IK_F9, ... - the format stays the same.

STEP 2 - bind the key (required):

  Open Documents\The Witcher 3\input.settings and paste the same line
  under the matching context:

      IK_Z=(Action=MCM_AR_Consent)

  Step 1 is required: without the ini declaration the game treats
  MCM_AR_Consent as an unknown action and strips the binding from
  input.settings the next time it writes the file.

STEP 3 - optional menu row:

  Copy the <Var ...> line from input.xml.optional into
  <The Witcher 3>\bin\config\r4game\user_config_matrix\pc\input.xml
  to get a "Consent (MCME AR)" row in Options -> Keybindings.

Done - restart the game or reload your save. The consent prompt shows
the key you bound.
